# Single CPO setup

The simplest pattern: your software operates charging for a single brand
under a single OCPI identity.

## Steps

1. **Pick an OCPI identity** — your `(country_code, party_id)` pair. The
   `country_code` is ISO-2 (e.g. `MX`, `BR`, `ES`). The `party_id` is 3
   ASCII alphanumeric characters of your choice. Common pattern: 3-letter
   abbreviation of your brand (e.g. `VOL` for Voltium).
2. **Receive Token A** from NetworkCore ops during onboarding
3. **Complete the [handshake](/ev-charging/charging-software/ocpi-integration#3-handshake-registration)** — receive Token C
4. **Push your locations** ([§5.1](/ev-charging/charging-software/ocpi-integration#51-push-a-location))
5. **Push your tariffs** ([tariffs page](/ev-charging/charging-software/tariffs))
6. **Push sessions + CDRs as they happen** ([§5.2](/ev-charging/charging-software/ocpi-integration#52-push-a-session-start) + [§5.3](/ev-charging/charging-software/ocpi-integration#53-post-a-cdr-final-billing-record))

That's it. The hub handles routing to DPs from there.
