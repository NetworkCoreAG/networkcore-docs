# For Distribution Partners

You bring drivers — through a mobility app, fleet platform, payment wallet,
OEM dashboard, or telematics system. The hub gives you access to a roaming
network of CPOs under a single OCPI integration plus the auxiliary surface
(risk, refunds, driver APIs) that real production deployments need.

## Start here

1. [**OCPI eMSP handshake**](/ev-charging/distribution-partners/handshake) — one-time setup
2. [**Pulling the location catalogue**](/ev-charging/distribution-partners/locations) — your in-app map data
3. [**Driver tokens**](/ev-charging/distribution-partners/tokens) — issuance + sync to the roaming network
4. [**Remote start/stop**](/ev-charging/distribution-partners/commands) — kicking off sessions from your app
5. [**Risk fingerprints**](/ev-charging/distribution-partners/risk-fingerprints) — POST device/account hashes for fraud defence
6. [**3DS recommendation**](/ev-charging/distribution-partners/threeds-recommendation) — auth-hint webhook for your PSP
7. [**Driver API**](/ev-charging/distribution-partners/driver-api) — embedded driver experience (history, invoices, push notifications)
8. [**Refunds & disputes**](/ev-charging/distribution-partners/refunds-disputes)
9. [**Reconciliation**](/ev-charging/distribution-partners/reconciliation)
