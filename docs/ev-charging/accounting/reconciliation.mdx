# Reconciliation

The day-to-day reconciliation cycle:

1. **Daily**: pull `/portal/v1/cpo/settlement?date_from=<yesterday>` + match each batch's `wire_reference` against the bank statement
2. **Weekly**: pull all CDRs for the period via OCPI `/emsp/cdrs?date_from=…` and confirm every CDR has a corresponding clearing record in the settlement reports
3. **Monthly**: export the `/export.zip` bundles and import into your ERP

## Drift detection

If you see a wire on your bank statement without a matching batch, or a
batch without a matching wire:

- Email [partner@networkcore.org](mailto:partner@networkcore.org) with the
  bank statement line item
- We'll match it within one business day

Most drift is one of two causes:

1. **Wire crossed a midnight UTC boundary** — sometimes the bank dates differently than we do
2. **Batched-vs-individual** — your bank may consolidate multiple batches into one wire if they hit the same day

## CDR ↔ Clearing record reconciliation

Every cleared CDR has exactly one clearing record. To audit:

```http
GET /portal/v1/cpo/clearing?date_from=2026-01-01&date_to=2026-01-31
```

The response includes each clearing record's `cdr_id`. Cross-reference
against the OCPI CDR list from `/emsp/cdrs`. Any CDR without a clearing
record means the clearing pipeline failed for that session — escalate.
