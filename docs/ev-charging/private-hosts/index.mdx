# For Private Hosts

A Private Host is any business that owns one or more EV chargers as a
secondary part of its operations: hotels, retail centres, offices, parking
operators, hospitals, universities, fleet depots, mixed-use developments.
The chargers exist to serve guests, customers, tenants or employees — but
most of the time they sit idle.

NetworkCore turns that idle capacity into incremental revenue without
changing how the chargers operate today. Your hardware stays. Your CPMS
stays. Your direct retail flow stays. NetworkCore runs alongside as an
additive demand channel and routes paying drivers from OEM apps, fleet
platforms, wallets, and other Distribution Partners to your existing plugs.

## What makes Private Hosts different from CPOs

Operationally, almost nothing changes for you:

| Concern | A CPO | A Private Host |
|---|---|---|
| Primary business | Running chargers | Running a hotel / retail / office, with chargers as a side service |
| Hardware ownership | Yours | Yours |
| CPMS in use | Yours | Yours (or none — Ampeco / Monta / Wallbox / ChargePoint, etc.) |
| Direct retail flow | Yours, untouched by NetworkCore | Yours, untouched by NetworkCore |
| OCPI integration | Required | Required |
| Settlement | Same SPEI / SEPA / local rails | Same — same protections |
| Revenue model | Public price - small network fee on incremental sessions | Same |

Architecturally you are a CPO from the hub's perspective — same OCPI
modules, same settlement infrastructure. The distinction is commercial,
not technical: a Private Host's signup goes through a different conversation
with the NetworkCore commercial team, and the fee model applies only to
incremental sessions routed via the network.

## Common questions

**How is this different from being a CPO?**
Operationally, almost nothing. Your hardware stays. Your CPMS stays. Your
direct retail flow stays. NetworkCore runs alongside as an additive
demand channel.

**How much can I earn? What's the fee model?**
You earn the public price set for your chargers, minus a small network
fee that applies only to incremental sessions NetworkCore routes to you.
No setup fees, no monthly fees.

**What happens to my existing direct customers?**
Nothing. NetworkCore only earns on incremental sessions we route to you.
Drivers already using your direct flow remain yours. We don't market to
or capture customers you already have — the No Cannibalisation Guarantee
is built into the model.

**Do I need to switch hardware or CPMS?**
No. NetworkCore is hardware-neutral and CPMS-agnostic. The OCPI
integration is the only requirement.

**What about chargers I'm still planning to install?**
Tell us — we can pre-onboard you so the moment hardware is live, demand
is too. The "Planning to install" option in the signup form flags this.
