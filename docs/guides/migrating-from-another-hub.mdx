# Migrating from another roaming hub

If you're currently integrated with Hubject, GIREVE, e-clearing.net, or
running direct OCPI bilaterals, here's the rough playbook for moving to (or
adding) NetworkCore as a hub.

## Phase 1 — Side-by-side handshake (1–2 days)

Add NetworkCore as **an additional OCPI endpoint** alongside your existing
hubs. Your CSMS likely already supports multiple `(country_code, party_id)`
peers — you just register one more.

1. Receive NetworkCore Token A from our ops team
2. Complete the [OCPI handshake](/ev-charging/charging-software/ocpi-integration#3-handshake-registration)
3. Push your existing locations / EVSEs / connectors to NetworkCore — same JSON shapes as your other hubs
4. Push your existing tariffs

At this point your locations show up in NetworkCore-connected DPs alongside
the other hubs. No driver-facing change.

## Phase 2 — Live sessions side-by-side (1 week)

Configure your CSMS to push sessions + CDRs to NetworkCore for any session
where the eMSP/DP is registered with NetworkCore. Continue pushing to your
other hubs for everyone else.

The hub determines DP membership at the CDR — you don't need to pre-filter.
If we don't have the DP, the CDR is rejected with `2004 Unknown token` and
you fall back to your other hub.

## Phase 3 — Cutover (your call)

Once you're confident in the NetworkCore integration, you have two paths:

- **Keep both hubs** — most CPOs do this for redundancy
- **Cut over fully** — drop your other hubs once NetworkCore covers the DPs you care about

We have no objection to either. We'd rather have you connected via both
hubs than not connected at all.

## Differences vs. typical OCPI hubs

| Concern | Typical hub | NetworkCore |
|---|---|---|
| Settlement | Bilateral with each eMSP, you handle invoicing | Hub handles clearing + settlement + invoicing end-to-end |
| Chargebacks | Your problem | Hub auto-assembles evidence pack + first-loss envelope absorbs the first 1% |
| Risk engine | None | Mechanism-based 17-point framework with explainable scoring |
| Tariff enforcement | Honour system | Locked tariffs — drift > 5% raises a fraud flag |
| Currency support | Often single-currency | Native multi-currency |
