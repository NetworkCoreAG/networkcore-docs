# Securing your account

Your NetworkCore partner portal account controls real money movement — settlement
destinations, payouts, disputes. Protect it with **two-factor authentication
(2FA)**: a one-time 6-digit code from an authenticator app, in addition to your
password.

2FA is **optional but strongly recommended**. (NetworkCore may require it for your
organization — if so, you'll be prompted to set it up the next time you sign in.)

## Turn on 2FA

1. Sign in to the portal and open **My profile** (top-right).
2. Under **Two-factor authentication**, click **Set up 2FA**.
3. In an authenticator app — Google Authenticator, Authy, 1Password, Microsoft
   Authenticator, etc. — scan the QR code (or type the manual key).
4. **Save your recovery codes.** You get 10 one-time codes, shown only once.
   Download them and keep them somewhere safe — they're how you get in if you
   lose your phone.
5. Enter the 6-digit code from the app and click **Enable 2FA**.

From then on, every sign-in asks for a code after your password.

## Signing in with 2FA

1. Enter your email and password as usual.
2. Enter the current 6-digit code from your authenticator app.

The code rotates every 30 seconds; if one is rejected, wait for the next one
(and check your phone's clock is set automatically).

## Lost your device?

- **Use a recovery code.** On the code screen, choose **"Use a recovery code"**
  and enter one of the codes you saved. Each works once.
- **Out of recovery codes?** Ask your organization's portal admin, or NetworkCore
  support, to reset your 2FA — you'll set it up again on your next sign-in.

## Turning 2FA off

In **My profile → Two-factor authentication**, click **Turn off** and confirm
with a current code. (If your organization requires 2FA, this option is disabled.)

## Good practice

- Use an authenticator app that backs up its seeds (Authy, 1Password) or store
  your recovery codes in a password manager, so a lost phone isn't a lockout.
- Don't share codes. NetworkCore will never ask you for a 2FA code by email,
  chat, or phone.
- Give every teammate their own login (**Team** in the portal) rather than
  sharing one — each can then hold their own 2FA.
